Dictate

Desktop / PWA: Join Teams, Zoom Workplace, Webex, or Google Meet in the app or Meet window, turn on live captions (Windows: Win+Ctrl+L also works), then click Send on Dictate Desktop.

Browser meetings: Load this extension in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave, Comet, Firefox, and other Chromium browsers. Join the meeting tab with live captions, then click Send.

Every caption is stored locally until you start a new transcript. Send forwards saved unsent captions, so older lines are not dropped as new ones arrive.
